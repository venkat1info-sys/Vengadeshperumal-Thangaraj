Autonomous Fire-Fighting Robot

Grade 8 STEM Curriculum Documentation

// Project Overview

The Autonomous Fire-Fighting Robot is an automated, mobile robotic vehicle designed as a hands-on learning project for Grade 8 students. Built on a custom, lightweight corrugated sheet chassis, the vehicle autonomously roams or patrols an area to seek out and extinguish small fires. When the onboard flame sensor detects a fire, the robot automatically navigates directly to the location of the hazard, halts at a safe distance, and activates an integrated water pump to extinguish the flame. This project serves as a middle school capstone that bridges theoretical physical sciences with applied robotics and coding.

// Working Process
  • Environmental Scanning: The robot operates as a mobile vehicle, patrolling an area while its flame sensor continuously scans the environment for infrared signatures.
  • Autonomous Movement: The moment a fire is detected, the microcontroller processes sensor inputs and automatically drives the vehicle directly toward the heat source.
  • Proximity Halt: To safeguard hardware and logic, the robot terminates movement at a preset, safe operational clearance metric.
  • Extinguishing Protocol: Once halted, the system activates the pressurized pump loop, streaming water continuously until sensor validation confirms fire suppression.
