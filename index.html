Autonomous Fire-Fighting Robot

Grade 8 STEM Curriculum Documentation

// Project Overview

The Autonomous Fire-Fighting Robot is an automated, mobile robotic vehicle designed as a hands-on learning project for Grade 8 students. Built on a custom, lightweight corrugated sheet chassis, the vehicle autonomously roams or patrols an area to seek out and extinguish small fires. When the onboard flame sensor detects a fire, the robot automatically navigates directly to the location of the hazard, halts at a safe distance, and activates an integrated water pump to extinguish the flame. This project serves as a middle school capstone that bridges theoretical physical sciences with applied robotics and coding.

// Working Process
  • Environmental Scanning: The robot operates as a mobile vehicle, patrolling an area while its flame sensor continuously scans the environment for infrared light signatures emitted by fire.
  • Autonomous Movement: The moment a fire is detected, the microcontroller processes the sensor data and automatically drives the vehicle forward, navigating directly toward the source of the flame.
  • Proximity Halt: To ensure safety, the robot is programmed to stop automatically at a predetermined, safe distance from the heat source.
  • Extinguishing Protocol: Once halted, the robot triggers the onboard water pump, shooting a stream of water at the flame. The pump runs continuously until the sensor confirms the fire is out, after which the robot resets.
